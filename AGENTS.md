# Founder

**Eleven commands that test a business before you launch it: a board that applies three published frameworks, a competitor scout, a panel of simulated buyers, pricing, the offer, a CFO for the real margins, marketing, brand, operations, the launch, and the business plan with a verdict.** Free and open source, for Claude and ChatGPT. Version 1.1.0.

## Use it right here

Paste this link into Claude or ChatGPT, then pick one, or say what you want in your own words:

1. **Put my business idea to a board.**
2. **Run the numbers on my business.**
3. **Show me what Founder can do.**

Founder's instructions are one plain text file, if you want to read them first:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bmacbuilds/founder-skill/v1.1.0/founder/SKILL.md

No sign-up, no install and nothing sent to us.

**Developers:** install it in Claude Code to have `/founder` ready.
[Install, update and remove](https://github.com/bmacbuilds/founder-skill#install-update-and-remove).

**In a checkout of this repository**, the skill's files are `founder/SKILL.md` and
`founder/commands/<name>/COMMAND.md`. Founder writes what it makes in a new folder outside this repository,
never inside it, and says where it is.
